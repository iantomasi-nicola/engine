Push-up sensor test

Records what this phone's motion sensors report while you do push-ups, so a rep counter can be checked on the real device. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere: copy the log and send it back.

How to test: press 1, put the phone where you want to try (in a hand, in a front pocket, strapped to the upper arm, on the floor under your chest), do a set, then type how many you really did and press 2. Repeat for each placement, with the place written in the box.

Press 1 to start.
